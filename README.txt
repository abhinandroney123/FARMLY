KEY IMPROVEMENTS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DEDICATED, SPACIOUS PAGES (NO CONGESTION):
   - 📊 Dashboard: Big 40px stat tiles, farm photo hero, 7-day egg production
     trend chart, visual feed silo gauge, and quick 1-click action buttons.
   - 🐔 Flock & Birds: Large bird counters, capacity tracker, batch age,
     lifetime mortality rate, and addition/loss timeline.
   - 🌾 Feed & Silo: Grain silo runway meter (kg and estimated days left),
     daily burn rate, low-stock warning banners, and restock logs.
   - 🥚 Egg Collection: Fresh farm eggs visual photo banner, morning/evening
     collection counts, Grade A vs cracked eggs, laying rate (%), and history.
   - 💉 Vaccines & Care: Clean cards for upcoming shots (Newcastle ND,
     Gumboro IBD, Deworming) with single-click "✓ Mark Done" completion.
   - 💰 Sales & Money: Revenue from eggs, birds, and manure against feed/medicine
     costs, with clear net operating margin.
   - ▤ Activity Log: Chronological farm diary with keyword search and CSV export.
   - ⚙️ Farm Settings: Edit farm profile, download one-click JSON backups,
     and restore anytime.

2. ENLARGED OFFICIAL LOGO:
   - Your uploaded rooster-heart FARMLY logo is displayed prominently at 90px
     with bold typography in the sidebar and featured on the dashboard hero.

3. REAL FARM PICTURES & WARM PASTEL THEME:
   - Beautiful photography of hens in green pasture, fresh brown farm eggs,
     and golden grains across module headers.
   - Light cream ivory canvas (#FAF7F2), soft pastel blue accents (#EEF5FB, #3876B8),
     and soft coral/red accents (#FDF2F0, #D85C49) matching the logo.

4. 100% OFFLINE & BROWSER SAVED:
   - All records are saved automatically in your browser's local storage.
   - No servers or complex setup required.
